# Shopify Sales & Customer Funnel Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Shopify sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, regional sales distribution, and payment gateway preferences. The dashboard provides actionable business insights by tracking revenue trends, customer retention, customer lifetime value, product category performance, and geographic sales distribution.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV Dataset
- Data Modeling

## Dataset
- 7,534 Sales Transactions
- 4,431 Unique Customers
- Multiple Product Categories
- Multiple Provinces and Cities
- Payment Gateway Information
- Customer Purchase History
- Sales, Tax, and Revenue Metrics

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance and revenue growth.
- Analyze customer retention and repeat purchase behavior.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate regional sales performance.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Improve customer lifetime value and purchase frequency.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.

## Key Insights
1. Generated total net sales of **$4.18M** from **7,534 transactions**.
2. Average Order Value reached **$562.6**, indicating strong customer spending.
3. Acquired **4,431 unique customers** during the analysis period.
4. **46% of customers** returned for repeat purchases.
5. Customer Lifetime Value reached **$943.6**, significantly exceeding AOV.
6. Purchase Frequency averaged **1.68 orders per customer**.
7. Running Shoes generated approximately **$1.5M**, making them the highest revenue-generating category.
8. Top 5 product categories contributed more than **90% of total sales revenue**.
9. Shopify Payments accounted for approximately **58.45% of total sales revenue**.
10. PayPal contributed approximately **17.62% of total revenue**.
11. Amazon Payments generated approximately **16.29% of total sales revenue**.
12. Peak sales periods outperformed the lowest periods by approximately **63%**, indicating strong seasonality and growth opportunities.

## Business Recommendations
- Increase investment in high-performing Running Shoe products.
- Launch customer loyalty and retention programs to improve repeat purchases.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing cities and regions.
- Optimize checkout experience through preferred payment gateways.
- Implement cross-selling and bundle strategies to increase AOV.
- Review and optimize low-performing product categories.
- Apply customer segmentation techniques (RFM Analysis) for personalized marketing campaigns.

## Business Impact
This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor revenue performance, improve customer retention, optimize product strategy, and identify high-growth regions. The insights generated support strategic decision-making and help drive sustainable business growth through data-driven actions.
